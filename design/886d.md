# Design 886d: Status views

This task gives four views as data: the global tableau, the contextual tableau, the work-blockage tree and the contributor work queue. It builds on the packages, the levels and the shared types of [design 493e](493e.md) and derives nothing: each rule below selects, counts and words over methods of `derive.Facts`. Eight choices shape it (D*n* is decision *n* under [Decisions at review](#decisions-at-review)). A tableau holds one cell per gate whatever the window, and `columns` states the window, the folds and a count per gate (D1). One set of tasks, the focus, fixes the window, and the rows of the detail level fix the counts, at every level (D2). A person's own tasks are the tasks assigned and the leaves contributed at next, parents among them (D3). A cell, a cause and a waiting item carry keys and typed facts, never a symbol string or a sentence (D5). The queue lists work as waiting for the four causes that hold a task where another person acts (D7). The tree rests on one relation, *holds*, and roots a requirement at the leaf that keeps it unmet (D10). A pin behind its trunk is one cause per link (D11). The brief is one object with the keys tabloio already reads (D13).

## The model

### Packages and words

The task adds three files to the public package `view` and three to `internal/views`, extends `views.Query` with `Window`, `Columns`, `Historical` and `Brief`, and adds four functions of 493e's shape: `Tableau`, `Context`, `Queue` and `Blockage`. The two tableaux share one type, `view.Tableau`. A function fails in no case; its caller, `snapshot.go` of 4ed9, guarantees what 493e states and three things more (A1). Each view carries `legend` and nests its levels as 493e's do; a row, a queue item and a cause nest a level the same way, through an embedded pointer.

Four words serve all four views, each a method of `builder`:

| Word                        | Is                                                                                                                                  |
|-----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| The **next gate** of a task | `f.Next(id, f.Status(id).Gate)`: the next applicable gate after the gate the status names, a parent's by its roll-up; `""` at the last gate |
| The **next junction**       | `f.Junction(id, next gate)`, nil without a next gate                                                                                  |
| A person's **own tasks**    | In display order: each task whose assignee is the person, and each leaf whose next junction is plain with the person as `Contributor.V` (D3) |
| **Proposed on the trunk**   | `!Authorisation(id).Authorised` and `Why` is `Determined` or `NoCommit`. Off the trunk, with no trunk and with no history, no task is (D9) |

### The two tableaux

`Tableau(f, q)` and `Context(f, q)`; type `view.Tableau`. `Context` takes `q.Task`, the task form, or `q.Person` with an empty `q.Task`, the person form. Three sets of tasks follow from the form, each in display order:

| View and form        | Focus                 | Rows at glance             | Rows at detail and provenance                                                                       |
|----------------------|-----------------------|----------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `tableau`            | Every task            | The root and its children  | Every task                                                                                          |
| `context`, a task    | The subtree under it  | The task and its children  | The subtree; the task's ancestors, labelled `spine`; the other children of its parent, `sibling`       |
| `context`, a person  | The person's own tasks | The own tasks             | The own tasks; each ancestor of one that is not own, `spine`; each other child of the parent of one, `sibling` |

A task that is both keeps the first label that applies: own or in the subtree, `""`; else `spine`; else `sibling`. A person with no own task gives `rows: []` (f394 A7).

**The window and the fold.** One rule, for every level of one view:

1. The **window** runs from `lo - n` to `hi + n`, clamped to the gates, where `lo` and `hi` are the least and the greatest index of the next gates of the tasks of the focus, and `n` is `q.Window`. With no next gate in the focus, the window is every gate.
2. With `q.Columns`, the gates it lists show, in gate order, and no window applies.
3. A gate that does not show folds, and each run of consecutive folded gates is one column (438a decision 1).
4. A gate's **count** is the number of tasks among the rows of the detail level whose status names it, a parent by its roll-up. A column's `count` is the sum over its gates and `by_gate` the count of each, on every column, folded or not (44bf A7).

The spine and the siblings therefore count and do not widen the window (D2). The counts and the columns are the same at glance, detail and provenance.

**The cell.** A row holds one cell per gate of `legend.gates`, in its order, whatever the columns (D1). With `at` the index of the gate the row's status names, `-1` when it names none, the cell at index `i` reads:

| Where                                          | `kind`   | Carries                                                    |
|------------------------------------------------|----------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| `i == at`                                      | `status` | `state` and `reason`, the keys of the status               |
| `i < at`, `q.Historical` off; and at `undefined` | `empty`  | Nothing                                                    |
| A not-applicable junction, elsewhere           | `exempt` | Nothing                                                    |
| Any other junction                             | `marks`  | `marks`, the keys of `f.Junction(id, gate).Marks()`        |

`historical` is `i < at`, whatever the parameter. `acts` is true on a `marks` cell of a leaf, at `i > at`, whose junction is plain with `q.Person` as contributor or reviewer: the work to come, never a parent's default (D4 gives a parent's marks).

**A row** holds the task; `under`, its parent's id; `depth`, the number of its ancestors that the data holds a row for; `parent`; `hidden`, the number of tasks beneath it with no row whose nearest ancestor with a row it is; `label`; `status`, through 493e's builder, so `status.from` names the direct child or the snapshot; and `leaf`, the task at the end of `f.Chain(id)` with the url of the subproject it is of, null for a chain of one.

**Provenance** adds, per row, the fields the task definition holds under the same names, for the row's task: `status_commit`, `own_commit`, `chain`, `junctions` with `from`, `sources` and `linkages`. It adds to the view `commands`: one line when the facts have a history, `git log --format='%H %as %ae %ce %s' <source commit> -- <dir>/.tableaux/status`.

### The contributor work queue

`Queue(f, q)`; type `view.Queue`. The tasks are the subtree under `q.Task`; the person is `q.Person`. `h` is `f.Handoff(id)` and `j` the next junction.

| `kind`          | An item for the person when                                                                                          | `gate`               |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------|
| `review`        | The task is a leaf, `h.Kind` is `Stated` and `h.Reviewer` is the person                                              | The next gate        |
| `authorisation` | The task is proposed on the trunk and the person is an email of `f.Authorities(id)`, or the owner                     | The status's gate    |
| `ready`         | The task is a leaf, `j` is plain with the person as `Contributor.V`, and no cause below holds                         | The next gate        |
| `reaffirmation` | The task is a leaf, its status is `Recorded` and `Status.Recorder` is the person                                     | The status's gate    |
| `waiting`       | As `ready`, and at least one cause holds                                                                              | The next gate        |

A leaf whose status is `Snapshotted` gives no `review`, `ready`, `reaffirmation` or `waiting` item: its next junction is recursive (D8). The **causes** of a waiting item, in this order (D7): `requirement`, one per condition of `f.Requires(id)` with `Due`, not `Met` and `Why` `Determined`, each with the `Requirement`; `reason`, when the status's reason is `blocked` or `overloaded`; `authorisation`, when the task is proposed on the trunk, with the nearest authority in `by`; `review`, when `h.Kind` is `Stated`, with the reviewer in `by`.

**Order.** The kinds as listed; within a kind display order; reaffirmations by the author time of `Status.Commit`, oldest first, then display order. `since` is `Status.Date`; `age`, on a reaffirmation, is the days from it to the date of the source commit, 0 without one (a3cc A5); `model` is `j.Model.V` on `review`, `ready` and `waiting`.

**Detail** adds to each item: `junction`, the next junction with `from`, and its `sources`; `task_references`; `requires`; the dependents in two lists, `unblocks`, each condition of `f.Dependents(id)` not `Met` whose `From` lies at or before the item's gate, and `also_required_by`, the rest; `parent_unblocks`, the same test over the dependents of the task's parent; `status` and `status_commit`; and `authorisation` on an authorisation item. An item with no next junction leaves `junction` null and `unblocks` empty. The gate's criteria stand in the legend.

**Provenance and the brief.** Provenance adds `brief`. With `q.Brief` the view holds `items: []` and `brief`: the object of [`model.md`](886d/model.md#view-the-work-queue) when the person's queue holds a `ready` or `waiting` item for that task at that gate, null otherwise (e4c7 A1). Without it, provenance is detail with `brief: null` (a3cc decision 4).

### The work-blockage tree

`Blockage(f, q)`; type `view.Blockage`. One relation builds the tree. Task X **holds** task Y, in this order of Y:

- **`parent`**: Y is X's parent, X is one of `Status(Y).Considered` and stands at the gate Y stands at. Y waits at its next gate.
- **`requirement`**: a condition of `f.Dependents(X)` names Y, with `Due`, not `Met`, `Why` `Determined`. Y waits at the condition's `To`.

The **causes**, each with the one person who acts:

| `kind`          | A cause when                                                                                                   | `gate`             | `resolver`, `act`                                                        |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `status`        | A leaf's status has the reason `blocked` or `overloaded` or the state `at_risk` or `stalled`                    | The status's gate  | The contributor of the next junction when it is plain, else `Status.Recorder`; `records` |
| `review`        | A leaf's `Handoff` is `Stated`                                                                                  | The next gate      | `Handoff.Reviewer`; `reviews`                                            |
| `authorisation` | A task, leaf or parent, is proposed on the trunk                                                                | The status's gate  | The nearest authority, the owner for the root; `authorises`              |
| `snapshot`      | A link's pin is behind: `f.Pin(link).Behind` is `Yes` for the link a `Snapshotted` leaf reads, or a due, unmet cross-project condition has `AtTrunk` `Yes`. One cause per link (D11) | The status's gate of `task` | The assignee of `task`, the first task the cause holds; `advances` |
| `requirement`   | A due, unmet local condition has a leaf beneath its originating task that nothing else holds (below); or a due, unmet cross-project condition has `AtTrunk` other than `Yes`, one cause per link and originating task | The cause task's next gate | The contributor of that junction, `""` when it is not plain; `contributes` |

The first four hold the task itself, `via: self`, at its next gate, and through it what it holds; a snapshot cause holds each such leaf and each task with such a condition, `via: requirement`. For a local condition, the **leaves beneath** the originating task are the task itself when it is a leaf, else the leaves beneath each considered child that stands at its gate. Such a leaf roots a `requirement` cause when it has none of the four causes above and no due, unmet condition of its own; otherwise the cause that holds the leaf reaches the dependent through it (D10). The cause holds what the leaf holds; the leaf itself is no held task. `mark` is the first of `Marks()` of the cause task's next junction for `status` and `requirement`, `""` without one; it is `reviewer`, `""` and `subproject` for the other three. For a cross-project requirement the status, the junction and the title read `Condition.Facts`.

**The tree.** At detail each cause holds `held`: per task it holds directly, the task, its gate, `via`, the `child` or the `Requirement`, the contributor at that gate on a leaf's plain junction, `also`, and `held` in turn, the `parent` edge first, then the `requirement` edges in the order of `f.Dependents`. A task on the path from the cause is not held again, so a cycle ends. `holds` counts the distinct tasks at every depth. `also` lists the other causes in view that hold the task, counted from 1 in `causes`.

**Selection and order.** With `q.Task` other than the root, a cause stays when its task, if of this project, or a task it holds lies in the subtree (0afa A7). With `q.Person`, it stays when the person is its resolver, or its task or a task it holds is one of the person's own. The causes run largest `holds` first; then by task in display order, a subproject's task last, by url, then id; then by kind as listed in VIEWS.md. `kinds` counts the causes that stay, in that order of kinds.

**Not yet due.** `not_due_count` and `not_due_unmet` count, at glance, the conditions with `Due` false and `Why` `Determined` of the tasks in the subtree, of the person's own tasks with a person; `not_due`, at detail, lists them by task with its status and its next gate.

**Provenance** adds, per cause, `status_commit`; `junction`, the cause task's next junction with `from`; `authorisation`; `linkage`; and `resolve`: `git commit --allow-empty --trailer 'Reviewed: <id> <gate>'` for a review, `git commit --allow-empty --trailer 'Authorised: <id>'` for an authorisation, empty otherwise. It adds to the view `commands`: `git log --format='%H %as %ae %ce %s' <source commit> -- <dir>/.tableaux/status <dir>/.tableaux/tasks`, with a history.

### Deterministic, the schema, the golden data

What 493e states holds here. `internal/views` also calls `time.Parse` on two dates for `age` and reads no clock. Every order above is total. The schema draft follows 493e's rule and joins `envelope.schema.yaml`; a tableau holds the same keys at glance and at detail, so the schema tells provenance from the two. The golden files follow 493e's naming: `tableau.detail.json`, `context-ben.detail.json`, `context-4e2b.glance.json`, `queue-ada.glance.json`, `queue-dan.glance.json`, `blockage.detail.json`.

### Kept and dropped

From [`prototype/886d`](../prototype/886d/README.md) the design keeps the four views as functions of one set of facts, the state at the gate the status names, the window around the next gates, and the kinds of the queue. It drops its loader and derivation, for `internal/derive`; the cell as a symbol string; the `folded` blocks, for `columns`; the flat list of held tasks, for the tree; `-cell` and `-order`; and the three causes it derives, for five.

## Conformance to the texts

The design implements four sections of [VIEWS.md](https://github.com/nbyoung/tableaux/blob/main/VIEWS.md): "Contributor work queue" with "The brief", "Work-blockage tree", "Global tableau" and "Contextual tableau", and of "What every view shares" the window, the historical junctions and the person parameter. Where a text is silent, or two disagree:

- **The window against [`tableau.md`](https://github.com/nbyoung/tableaux/blob/main/docs/mockups/tableau.md) and [`context.md`](https://github.com/nbyoung/tableaux/blob/main/docs/mockups/context.md).** A trial over the tooling plan at `3cdae52`, the mockups' commit, gives each window, fold and count the two draw: `❔ ×0` with 📝 to 🚀; `❔…📝 ×29` and `🚀 ×0` at window 0; 📝 to 📏 with `❔` and `🔗…🚀` for `2034` and for the owner; 39 and 34 cells where a person acts; `Method (31)` at glance. Two things differ and stay as drawn: `context.md` writes `Views (3)` where the ruling of 2026-10-06 counts 23, and twelve cells of three parents (D4).
- **Whose next gates the window spans.** VIEWS.md counts the spine and the siblings among the tasks in view of the person form; `context.md` spans the person's ten tasks and says of the task form that the spine and the siblings "do not widen it". tableaud's prototype `db74` (finding 6) reads the first and tablotui's `f394` the second. D2.
- **A person's tasks.** VIEWS.md's example calls `4e2b`, which Ben is assigned, his spine; `context.md` counts the owner's three parents as the owner's own. D3.
- **A recursive next junction.** VIEWS.md says such a task "is no item for anyone"; `queue.md` lists four reaffirmations of such tasks. The text stands (D8).
- **The queue's waiting causes.** VIEWS.md names an unmet requirement and two reasons. D7 adds two.
- **A child that holds its parent.** VIEWS.md does not state it; `blockage.md` proposes it and a3cc A4 reads it. D10 fixes when.
- **Four keys.** VIEWS.md names `blocked`, `overloaded`, `at_risk` and `stalled`; SYNTAX.md leaves the states and reasons to each project's `gates.yaml`. D12.
- **`blockage.md` at `3cdae52` shows no cause.** The trial gives its ten requirements not yet due, eight unmet. It also gives four snapshot causes there, because the subprojects' trunks have moved since the mockup: the pins read behind today.
