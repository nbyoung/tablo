# Design dada: Audit

The Audit turns what the Validator and the Derivation already know into findings a person acts on. It wraps every diagnostic of the envelope in a record that adds the act that resolves it, the person who takes it and the facts of the Derivation it rests on, and it raises the three disagreements that no rule states: a proposed task, a stale status and a junction nobody but its contributor reviews. It reads no history and derives no fact; every fact comes from a method of `derive.Facts`, and every rule finding from a diagnostic it carries unchanged. The owner reviews the design on 2026-10-07 and rules on its eight decisions; this text agrees with each (D*n* is decision *n* under [Decisions at review](#decisions-at-review)). The Audit raises three kinds, and a parameter, `minimum`, hides the mildest by default (D1). An act is one of ten words (D2); four history rules resolve by nothing, and such a finding lists as information once its junction is historical or its commit is old (D3). One table names who resolves each rule (D4). A status is stale only when a commit records it, it is not complete and no subproject states it (D5). Off the trunk the Audit reports the tasks a branch changes (D6). The library reads no clock and requires the date (D7). The record splits: the message, the kind and the sentence stay tablo's words, and the action and the facts are an act and typed facts in the shared types of `view` (D8).

## The model

### The packages

| Path             | Holds                                                                    | Imports                                  |
|------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| `view`           | `audit.go`: `Audit`, `Finding`, `FindingFacts`, the data a front end names | Nothing (493e decision 1)                |
| `internal/audit` | `Run`, `Report`, `Finding`, `Facts` and the table of the 81 rules        | `model`, `history`, `derive`, `validate` |

The functions build no data of `view`: a finding of `internal/audit` holds pointers to the Derivation's own values, and the audit view (8ed1) writes `view.Audit` from a `*Report` through the builders of `internal/views`, so that a junction, a status or a commit has one shape in every view (493e decision 5, A3). `internal/audit` imports `validate` for `Rules` alone, since a rule's title is the finding's kind, and `time` for `time.Parse` alone.

```go
// Input is what one audit reads.
type Input struct {
	Facts       *derive.Facts      // the facts of the project in view
	Diagnostics []model.Diagnostic // the Loader's and the Validator's, as snapshot.go sorts them
	Now         string             // the date staleness counts from, YYYY-MM-DD; required
	Stale       int                // the age in days; 0 is DefaultStale, 7
}

// Run audits a project. It returns a *DateError for a Now that is no date,
// and nil with no error when in.Facts is nil or refuses.
func Run(in Input) (*Report, error)

func (r *Report) Keep(keep func(*Finding) bool) *Report // the findings keep accepts
func (r *Report) Rows(minimum model.Severity) []Row     // the findings at or above minimum, joined
func (r *Report) Counts() Counts                        // by listed severity, one per finding, whatever the minimum
func (r *Report) Most() *Resolving                      // who the most findings ask an act of
func (r *Report) Silent() []Silence                     // the kinds examined that no finding has
```

[`dada/model.md`](dada/model.md) declares the rest. `Run` does six things in order: it checks the date; it wraps each diagnostic by its row of the table; it raises `proposed` for each task, `stale` for each leaf and `sole_review` for each junction of each leaf, in display order; it gives every finding its facts, commits and commands; it demotes; and it sorts.

### What the Audit adds, finding by finding

The line stands as `8118` decisions 1 and 13 and `27a3` decision 8 draw it: a finding with an id in RULES.md is the Validator's diagnostic, and the Audit raises only what has no id. No finding has two owners. The first six rows are the task description's list.

| Finding                                         | Raised by                | The Audit adds                                                     | From `derive.Facts`                                   |
|-------------------------------------------------|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| A proposed task                                 | The Audit, `proposed`    | The whole finding                                                  | `Authorisation`, `Authorities`, `Owner`, `Trunk`, `Order` |
| A status past an unreviewed junction (S11)      | The Validator            | `review` by the junction's reviewer; the status's deciding commit  | `Junction`, `Status`                                  |
| An unmet requirement (R9, R13)                  | The Validator            | `await` by whoever works on the originating task; `move_pin` by the assignee | `Requires`, `Chain`, `Leaf`, `Next`, `Junction` |
| A stale status                                  | The Audit, `stale`       | The whole finding                                                  | `Status`, `Log`, `Next`, `Junction`                   |
| A junction nobody but its contributor reviews   | The Audit, `sole_review` | The whole finding, as information (D1)                             | `Junctions`, `Status`, `Index`, `ByAuthorisation`, `Authorities` |
| A subproject that does not resolve (J8, J9), a pin or `commit` off its place (J13 to J17) | The Validator | `checkout` by nobody or `revise` by the keeper; `move_pin` by the assignee; the linkage | `Junction` and its `Snapshot` |
| A trailer that names nothing (H1)               | The Validator            | `none`, the commit's author                                        | `Log`                                                 |
| A review from the wrong hand (H2)               | The Validator            | `review` by the reviewer until a review accepts, then `none`       | `Accepted`, `Junction`                                |
| A model outside the one stated, or none (H3, H6) | The Validator           | `none`, the commit's author; the model check                       | `Events` and each `Reading`                           |
| A hand-off the history implies, or a stale one (H4, H5) | The Validator    | `record_handoff` or `clear_handoff` by the contributor             | `Handoff`, `Junction`, `Status`                       |
| An undetermined trunk (P5)                      | The Validator            | `revise` by the owner; P5 stands for every proposed task           | `Owner`, `Trunk`                                      |
| Every other rule                                | The Loader, the Validator | `revise` by the keeper of the file                                | `Authorities`, `Status`, `Owner`                      |

The Audit derives nothing itself. It compares two dates with `Now` and two places of `Index`, and it finds the requirement or the link a diagnostic concerns by the diagnostic's position, since a diagnostic carries no index (A5). `Unread`, `Reviews` and each event's `Effect` reach it only through the Validator's diagnostics.

### The three kinds

**A proposed task**, a warning. For each task of `Order()`, with `a := Authorisation(id)`:

- No history, or no trunk (`Trunk().Tip` is empty): no finding. Without a trunk rule P5 already says that every task reads as proposed, so one fault gives one finding.
- On the trunk: a finding when `a.Authorised` is false, with the deciding commit, or with `NoCommit` for a task file that no commit of the trunk holds, as an untracked file in the working tree is.
- Off the trunk (`a.Why` is `OffTrunk`): a finding only when `a.Differs`, so a branch reports the tasks it adds or changes and stays silent about the plan it inherits (D6), as the queue and the blockage tree read it (886d decision 9).

The resolver is the task's nearest authority in view, since a new `Authorised:` commit reads the tree as it then stands; for the root it is the judge of the deciding commit, the outgoing owner (`27a3` decision 3). An authorised task whose file has an uncommitted edit gives no finding: the edit is no commit yet.

**A stale status**, a warning. For each leaf of `Order()`, with `s := Status(id)`: a finding when `s.Kind` is `Recorded`, `s.Commit` is not nil, `s.Uncommitted` is false, `s.State` is not `complete`, and the days from `s.Date` to `Now` exceed `Stale`. Both are calendar dates, so the count takes no zone: a status dated 2026-09-22 is stale at three days on 2026-09-26 and not on 2026-09-25. `Status.Date` already is the newest of the file's change and a `Reaffirmed:` commit. The resolver is the recorder, the person whose queue lists the reaffirmation (886d, the queue's fourth kind). A leaf with no status file has recorded nothing to confirm; a snapshot's date is the subproject's, and that project's own audit reports it (`8118` decision 11, 886d decision 8); a complete task waits for nothing (D5).

**A sole review**, information. For each leaf of `Order()` and each `j` of `Junctions(id)`: a finding when `j.Kind` is `Plain`, the junction is not [historical](#the-demotion), `j.Reviewer.V` is `""` or equals `j.Contributor.V`, and the authorisation does not stand as its review. One kind covers the two cases of README.md#roles, and the Junction fact tells them apart: the contributor reviews its own work, an agent where the junction states a model; or a person's plain junction states no reviewer and has none. Its act is `none`: the plan allows both. Its resolver is the task's nearest authority, the owner for the root, who learns where work passes a gate on one word. A parent gives none, since its junctions are defaults; nor does a recursive or a not-applicable junction.

The junction the authorisation reviews is no sole review: there an authority accepts the work, and where none does `proposed` already reports the task (README.md#status, "The `defined` junction is the exception"; RULES.md, S11). The Audit names no gate key for it. It reads the Derivation's fact, `Review.ByAuthorisation`, through one method more, `Facts.ByAuthorisation(id, gate) bool`, which lands with this task as `LastChange` lands with `493e` (A9): `Accepted(id, gate)` on `main` carries the fact, and returns nil for a junction that states no reviewer, which is one of the two cases here.

[`dada/words.md`](dada/words.md#the-three-kinds) gives each field and each message of the three.

### The demotion

A junction is **historical** when it is one of the task's historical junctions, VIEWS.md's term for the cells before the state cell ("What every view shares"): `Index(gate) >= 0` and `Index(gate) < Index(Status(id).Gate)`. Every junction of a task whose state is `complete` is historical too. A parent reads by its roll-up and a snapshot by the task it reads. The junction at the gate the status names is not historical, though in the method's word the task has passed that gate; the design uses "passed" in that sense alone. `complete` is the one state the Audit names (886d decision 12).

A finding whose act is `none` and whose junction is historical lists as `information`: `Finding.Severity` is `Information`, `Demoted` is true, and it carries the status that says so. It sorts, joins and counts by the listed severity. Its `Diagnostic` keeps the rule's own severity, so the envelope's `diagnostics`, the exit code and `tablo validate` do not change (D3).

A finding with the act `none` and no junction demotes by one of two rules, with the same effect. Where it names a task and **no gate** of the project, as `Reviewed: 9f31 nowhere`, it demotes when that task is complete. Where it names **no task** of the project, as an H1 may, it demotes once its commit is older than the stale threshold: when the days from the author date of the commit its diagnostic names to `Now` exceed `Stale`, the count a stale status takes. With no such commit in the pass it keeps the rule's severity. The Audit guesses no junction.

### The minimum

The audit takes one parameter more, `minimum`: the lowest severity it lists, `error`, `warning` or `information`, by default `warning`. `Rows(minimum)` lists the rows at or above it; `Counts`, `Most` and `Silent` read every finding in view, so the three counts say what the list hides. By default the audit therefore lists neither a sole review, nor H4, nor a demoted finding, and counts each. It asks:

- **Of `4ed9`**: a field `Params.Minimum`, `""` for `warning`; the flag `--minimum <severity>` on `audit` alone, in `fields`, `viewTable`, `Check`, `Narrow`, the command table and `usage.txt`; a `*UsageError` for another word; and the resolved word in the envelope's `params`, an `enum` the schema requires of `audit` beside `stale` and `now` (A2).
- **Of `8ed1`**: `views.Query` gains `Minimum` beside `Stale` and `Now`; the view selects with `Keep`, counts, and then lists with `Rows` (A3).
- **Of the front ends**: each offers the parameter, shows the three counts whatever it lists, and says so where a count exceeds the rows shown (A8).

### The clock

The package reads no clock, and `Now` is required: `Run` returns a `*DateError` for an empty `Now` and for one that is no date, before it reads the facts, and `snapshot.go` returns it as a `*UsageError` (D7). A live front end that forgets the date gets an error and not an audit that understates staleness. `Stale` 0 means seven days, as VIEWS.md's Q6 rules. The command keeps its default as `4ed9` decision 7 accepts it: `cli.Run` fills `--now` with the host's local date. The static export passes the commit's author date itself (6160 A9). With no history nothing is stale, and the date is still required.

### The act and the resolver

An **act** is the kind of an action, a closed list of ten words, an `enum` in the schema. Eight are the list of VIEWS.md#audit: `authorise`, `review`, `reaffirm`, `record_handoff`, `clear_handoff`, `revise`, `move_pin`, `checkout`. Two are new (D2): `await`, for an unmet requirement, which resolves when the originating task passes a gate and by no edit of the file that reports it; and `none`. The word is not `advance`, since 886d's `advances` names the act on a pin or a gate. The Audit words no action: a front end words it from the act and the typed facts.

`none` is the act of a sole review, of H1, H3, H6 and of an H2 whose junction a review has since accepted. Each of the four rules reports a commit, the files at a commit judge the commit (`27a3` decision 2), and a commit does not change: no later commit and no edit of the plan removes the finding. Their resolver is the commit's author, who learns of it (D3). The audit mockup draws "Revise the file: state the model that ran" for H3; under decision 2 that revision changes the next commit's reading and leaves the finding. The mockup stays as drawn.

The **resolver** is one email, or empty where the files name nobody. Each rule names a role ([`dada/rules.md`](dada/rules.md)), and a role resolves as follows (D4); `id` and `gate` are the diagnostic's.

| Role        | The email                                                                                                                              |
|-------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| owner       | `Owner()`                                                                                                                              |
| authority   | The first of `Authorities(id)`; the owner for the root                                                                                 |
| assignee    | The task's assignee as its file states it; the owner for an id that names no task or a task with none                                  |
| keeper      | By the diagnostic's file. `tasks/<id>.yaml` of a task of the project: the authority, since an authority's commit is proposal and acceptance at once. `status/<id>.yaml` of a task of the project: the recorder. Any other file, or none: the owner |
| recorder    | `Status(id).Recorder`; when it is empty, the contributor at `Next(id, Status(id).Gate)`                                                |
| contributor | `Junction(id, gate).Contributor.V` of a plain junction; else the assignee                                                              |
| reviewer    | `Junction(id, gate).Reviewer.V`; else the assignee                                                                                     |
| author      | The author's email of the commit the diagnostic names; the owner when the pass holds no such commit                                    |
| origin      | Of the condition the diagnostic stands at, in the originating project's facts (`Condition.Facts`): the first status of `Chain(Origin)` whose task is a leaf there gives the contributor of that leaf's next junction when it is plain, else the leaf's assignee. With no condition or no such leaf: the assignee |
| nobody      | The empty string: `checkout` falls to whoever holds the clone                                                                          |

The **condition** a diagnostic stands at is the entry of `Requires(id)` whose `Entry.Node`, or whose `Subproject` node, `URL` node or `Commit` node, has the diagnostic's position. The **link** a diagnostic concerns is `Junction(id, gate).Snapshot.Link` when it names a gate, and that condition's `Link` when it names none.

### The finding record

The record splits (D8). Four fields stay tablo's words, so that the audit and `tablo validate` say one finding one way: `message`, the Validator's diagnostic byte for byte, or the kind's own text; `kind`, the rule's `Title` from `validate.Rules`; and `sentence` and `source`, the method's own text and its address, absolute. Nothing else is worded. In place of a worded action a finding carries `act` and `commands`; in place of worded facts, the typed facts: a junction, a status, a requirement, an authorisation, a linkage with its pin, a model check and the commits, each in the shared type of `view`. [`dada/words.md`](dada/words.md#the-typed-facts) states, per rule and per kind, which facts a finding carries and the method of `derive.Facts` that gives each.

**The data.** `view.Audit` is one flat object, and its levels nest as 493e's do (493e decision 2). Glance holds `legend`, `errors`, `warnings`, `information`, `most` and `findings`, each finding with `key`, `rule`, `severity`, `demoted`, `kind`, `tasks`, `gate`, `act` and `resolver`. Detail adds `silent`, and `files` and `message` per finding. Provenance adds `sentence`, `source`, `facts`, `commits` and `commands` per finding. `facts` holds one object per task of the row that has a fact, with the six keys always present and null where the finding carries none. [`dada/audit.json`](dada/audit.json) is the data of one audit at provenance.

**Beside the diagnostic.** The envelope's `diagnostics` stay as the Validator sorts them, and the audit changes neither them nor the exit code (`4ed9`, the exit codes). Each diagnostic gives exactly one finding, which holds a pointer to it and copies its code, task, gate and message, so a consumer joins the two lists on those. `files` holds `.tableaux/` and `Pos.File`, the envelope's `path`. The three kinds have an empty `rule` and a `key` of their own; for a rule finding `key` equals `rule`. The Audit raises no finding under a rule id (`8118` A10).

**Order.** `Findings` sorts by: the listed severity, errors first, then warnings, then information; the kind, in the order of `validate.Rules`, then `proposed`, `stale`, `sole_review`; the gate, none first, then the order of `gates.yaml`, then any other key by bytes; the task, none first, then display order, then any other id by bytes; the oldest commit, none first, then the oldest first; the diagnostic's file, line and column; the message; the trailer. The sort is stable over the order `Run` raises in.

**Rows.** `Rows` joins findings that agree in `key`, the listed severity, `gate`, `message`, `act` and `resolver`. A finding with a task joins the first such row that does not hold that task; a finding with no task never joins. The view writes a row's tasks in order, each file once, the facts of each task, and each commit once, the oldest first, with the commands of the first finding. So the twenty tasks that require one task at one gate make one row, and two commits that carry one unread trailer make two.

**Counts and most.** `Counts` counts the findings of `Findings`, not the rows and not the rows listed: one per task a kind hits and one for a finding with no task, so the three counts sum to the diagnostics, the proposed tasks, the stale statuses and the sole reviews. `Most` counts, per resolver, the findings whose act is not `none`, and skips the empty resolver: a finding nothing resolves is no work, and the sole reviews would otherwise name the top authority of every plan.

**Silent.** `Silent` lists, in the order above, each kind the run examines and no finding of the report has, hidden or not: the rules VIEWS.md#audit names (P5, R9, R12, R13, J8, J9, J13 to J17, H1 to H6), then `proposed`, `stale` and `sole_review`, each with its key, rule and kind. A run examines a rule of `validate.TierHistory` only with a history, `proposed` only with a trunk, and the other two always. It words no reason for a silence.

### Subprojects, errors, and what is deterministic

The Audit reports the project in view. It follows no link: a subproject's proposed tasks, stale statuses and sole reviews are findings of its own audit, as its diagnostics are (`8118` decision 11). `Run` fails for the date alone and never panics; a diagnostic whose task, gate or commit the project lacks takes the fallback its role names. With a refusal it returns nil, and `data` is null as `27a3` decision 6 rules. The same input gives equal reports on every host: every list has a stated order, no map is ranged over unsorted, and the package reads neither the clock nor the environment.

## Conformance to the texts

The Audit implements VIEWS.md#audit: its Data paragraph, finding by finding in the table above; its `stale` parameter and the seven days; "each finding names the action that resolves it and the person who takes it", as an act. It implements README.md#proposed-and-authorised-tasks for the proposed task, README.md#status, "Date and recorder", for the stale one and README.md#roles for the sole review, and it shows the sentences of RULES.md. It draws no view. Where a text is silent, a mockup is stale or two texts disagree, the design reads as follows.

- **The acts.** VIEWS.md lists eight and gives an unmet requirement none; the example's "Resolves when" column reads "`e9c6` passes design". D2 adds `await` and `none`.
- **"The action that resolves it"** has no answer for H1, H3 and H6 (D3). An old project keeps every such finding for as long as its history stands, as information once its junction is historical or, with no task, once its commit is older than the stale age.
- **An agent with no human reviewer** is in the task's description and in no rule and no sentence of VIEWS.md. README.md#junctions lets an agent review its own work, and README.md#roles gives a person's plain junction no reviewer. D1 reports both as information, and VIEWS.md#audit gains the kind and the parameter `minimum`.
- **Which status is stale.** VIEWS.md says "a status whose date is older than an age" and does not exclude a complete one, a snapshot or a leaf with no file (D5).
- **Who reaffirms.** PLAN.md gives reaffirming to the contributor and VIEWS.md lists reaffirmations for "statuses the person recorded". The design takes the recorder (D4).
- **Off the trunk.** README.md says every task is proposed there. The fact stands in `Authorisation`; the finding is narrower (D6).
- **The junction at `defined`.** README.md#status and RULES.md's S11 name `defined` themselves: the authorisation stands as its review. The Audit lists no sole review there, by the Derivation's fact and no key of its own (D1). `886d` decision 12 stands: its summary of the keys the method fixes omits `defined`, which the method names for this one exception.
- **The audit mockup.** `docs/mockups/audit.md` draws a "Silent" section with a worded reason per rule, "As above" in an action cell, H3 at `defined` for an edit of a task file, an alternative in the action of S11 and `status/<id>.yaml` for a row. The data holds the silent kinds without reasons, an act per finding and every file; a front end words the action. The mockup stays as drawn.
- **The corpus.** No entry has an `audit` section and none states a stale status or a sole review, so the corpus fixes the proposed task alone, as the fact `authorisation.state` (RULES.md, "Derived facts, not rules"): `weather-station` (`3c5d`, and every task on `sensor-board`), `trunk-inferred` and `trunk-stated` (`c3d7`), `trunk-undetermined`. The findings the Audit wraps have their entries in RULES.md. The corpus exercises no demotion, no self-review under H4, no H2 that a later review accepts, no J8 for a missing clone and no valid project with a complete status; tests T7 to T9 and T13 build these.

## Decisions at review

1. **Two kinds, and none for an agent that reviews its own work.** The Audit raises `proposed` and `stale`, both warnings, and says of a self-review only what the action of H4 and the Junction fact say. The alternative follows the task's description and reports, as information, every junction where an agent contributes and no other person reviews. Binds `8ed1`. Decided by the owner on 2026-10-07: three kinds, and a filter by severity. `proposed` and `stale` stand. A third kind, `sole_review`, is information: a leaf's plain junction, at or after the gate its status names, where nobody but the contributor reviews. It covers an agent whose reviewer is itself and a person's plain junction with no reviewer, told apart by the Junction fact and not by two kinds; its act is `none` and its resolver the task's nearest authority, the owner for the root. The audit takes a parameter `minimum`, the lowest severity it lists, `warning` by default; the filter applies to the rows listed and the counts cover all three severities. The default hides H4, and the owner accepts that. Binds `4ed9`, `8ed1`, the three front ends and VIEWS.md#audit. Decided by the owner on 2026-10-07, at the second review: the test stands, a plain junction whose reviewer is empty or equals the contributor, at a junction that is not historical. The junction at `defined` is no sole review: the method names `defined` itself (README.md#status; RULES.md S11), the authorisation stands there as the review, and the Audit excludes it by the Derivation's fact, `ByAuthorisation`, and names no gate key; `886d` decision 12 stands. `Most` skips the findings whose act is `none`, and the three counts still cover every finding. Binds `27a3` (A9).
2. **Ten acts.** The eight of VIEWS.md#audit, as the keys `authorise`, `review`, `reaffirm`, `record_handoff`, `clear_handoff`, `revise`, `move_pin` and `checkout`, with one for R9 and `none`. Accepted by the owner on 2026-10-07, with one word changed: the act of an unmet requirement is `await`, not `advance`, since `886d` uses `advances` for a pin or a gate. The acts are an `enum`.
